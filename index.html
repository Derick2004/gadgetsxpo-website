Our story

Written in the stars

From a university corridor in 2024 to this very night — a small map of how we got here.

2024

Freshmen year, first hello

A new campus, a sea of new faces — and somehow, the one person I could never stop noticing found her way into my story.

Every day since

A letter, for you

My dearest Snothando,

I could have bought you anything this year, but I wanted to give you something no one else could ever give you — a night sky of your own, with every star arranged in your name.

We met in our first year, when everything was new and slightly frightening, and you made it all feel lighter. Since then, you have become my favourite hello, my hardest goodbye, and every good thing in between.

If you ever forget how rare you are, come back here. Scroll up, read it all again, and remember: someone loves you with the whole of this sky — and always will.

Happy birthday, my Princess Diana.

— Mukelwa

A constellation of reasons

Why I love you

01

The way you laugh before you even finish the joke.

02

03

04

Birthday wishes

Tonight, the sky grants five

May this year love you as loudly as I do.
May every dream you whisper about quietly come true.
May your laughter never lose its spark.
May you always see yourself the way I see you — magic.
And may every wish you make tonight find its way home.

So without further delay

Happy Birthday,
Princess Diana

Make a wish before the star fades. One of them is already mine — that every birthday of yours is brighter than your last.
